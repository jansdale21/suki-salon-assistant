## Nail notes
- Gel polish usually lasts 2–3 weeks. Free fix within 3 days if a nail chips or lifts (not for broken nails).
- The salon uses a new file and buffer for every customer, and metal tools are cleaned and sterilised after each use.
- Customers may bring their own polish; the price stays the same.
- The salon does not treat ingrown toenails, nail fungus or infected nails. Customers with these should see a doctor; the technician may skip the affected nail.
- No acrylic extensions are offered (soft gel only).
