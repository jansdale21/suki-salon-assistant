## Patch test policy (important)
- First-time colour customers, and anyone who has not coloured with us in the last 6 months, must do a free skin patch test at least **48 hours before** the colour appointment. It takes 5 minutes at the front desk.
- Without a patch test, the salon will not do the colour service.
- Customers with known allergies to hair dye, or who are pregnant or under medical treatment, should consult their doctor first. The salon does not give medical advice.

## Deposits for long services

- The deposit is refundable if cancelled at least 24 hours before; see the cancellation policy.
