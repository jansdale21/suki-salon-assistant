# SALON KNOWLEDGE — About, hours and location

> All details in this knowledge base are invented for a student project (SAM10). Suklay & Kulay is not a real business.

## About us
- Name: Suklay & Kulay Beauty Studio ("suklay" means comb, "kulay" means colour).
- Tagline: "Ganda na swak sa budget" (beauty that fits your budget).
- Opened: 2019. A family-run neighbourhood salon with 6 chairs, 2 nail stations and 1 private treatment room.
- Services: hair (cut, styling, colour, treatments, rebond and keratin), nails (manicure, pedicure, gel, nail art), brows and lashes, waxing, and event makeup.

## Location
- Ground floor, Sampaguita Arcade, 18 Mabini Street, Barangay Bagong Liwanag, Lungsod ng Malaya (a fictional city).
- Landmark: beside the Bagong Liwanag public market, across from the basketball court.
- Parking: 4 free slots in front of the arcade on a first-come basis. Motorcycle parking at the side.
- The salon is on the ground floor with a ramp; wheelchair accessible. One accessible restroom.
