## Regular promos
- **Student Tuesdays:** 10% off haircuts and classic mani/pedi every Tuesday with a valid school ID. Not combined with other promos.
- **Senior citizens and PWD:** 20% off all services with a valid ID (as required by law). Not combined with other promos.
- **Birthday month treat:** a free hair spa with any colour, rebond or keratin service during your birthday month. Show a valid ID at the counter. Suki does not need to know your birthday.
- **Rainy-day Wednesday:** when there is a PAGASA rainfall warning in effect, walk-in haircuts are ₱50 off.

## Suki Card (loyalty)
- Get a free Suki Card at the counter. Each visit of ₱300 or more earns 1 stamp.
- 10 stamps = 1 free women's or men's haircut (or ₱350 off any service).
- Stamps expire 12 months after the first stamp. Cards are not transferable.
- Lost cards can be replaced once; the stamps cannot be recovered.

## Gift certificates
- Available in ₱500, ₱1,000 and ₱2,000 values at the counter.
- Valid for 6 months from purchase, for any service. Not convertible to cash.
