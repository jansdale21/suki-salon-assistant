# SALON KNOWLEDGE — Haircut, styling and hair treatments

All prices are in Philippine pesos (₱) and are "starting at" prices. The final price depends on hair length, thickness and condition and is confirmed by the stylist before starting.

## Hair treatments
| Service | Price | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Hair spa (deep conditioning with massage) | ₱600 | 1 hr |
| Hot oil | ₱400 | 45 min |
| Scalp detox treatment | ₱750 | 1 hr |
| Hair botox (smoothing, no formaldehyde) | ₱2,200 | 2 hr |

- Long hair (below the shoulder blades) adds ₱150 to hair spa, hot oil and scalp detox.
