# SALON KNOWLEDGE — Booking and salon policies

## How to book (customers do this themselves; Suki cannot book)
1. Walk in and book at the front desk, or
2. Email hello@suklaykulay.example with the service you want and two preferred dates and times. The front desk replies within 1 business day to confirm.
- Walk-ins are welcome for haircuts, blow-dry and classic nails when a chair is free. Saturdays and the days before holidays are the busiest; booking is recommended.
- Colour, rebond, keratin, Brazilian blowout, perm, lash extensions, bridal and makeup services are by appointment only.
- Suki (the chat assistant) cannot see, make, change or cancel bookings.

## Arrival and lateness
- Please arrive 10 minutes early for colour and chemical services (for the consultation).
- Grace period: 15 minutes. After 15 minutes late, the salon may shorten the service or move the appointment to the next free slot.

## Cancellation and no-show
- Cancel or reschedule at least **24 hours** before the appointment, by email or at the counter, for a full deposit refund (or move the deposit to the new date).
- Cancelled less than 24 hours before, or a no-show: the ₱500 deposit is forfeited.
- After 2 no-shows, future appointments need full prepayment.

## Children
- Children under 12 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian for the whole service.
- Chemical services (colour, rebond, perm, keratin) are not done on children under 16.

## Satisfaction and redo policy
- Haircut re-fix: free within 7 days.
- Colour adjustment: free within 7 days if the colour is clearly different from what was agreed (not for a change of mind).
- Gel fix: free within 3 days for chipping or lifting.
- Refunds are not given for completed services; the salon offers a redo instead.

## Other policies
- Pets are not allowed inside, except service animals.
- Free Wi-Fi for customers (ask the front desk for the password).
- No smoking or vaping inside or at the entrance.
- The salon is not responsible for lost items; please keep valuables with you.
- The salon does not sell hair extensions or wigs, and does not do hair extension installation.
