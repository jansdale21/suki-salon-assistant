# SALON KNOWLEDGE — Aftercare tips and frequently asked questions

These are the salon's general care tips, not medical advice. For irritation, burning, rashes, swelling or hair loss after any service, stop using products and see a doctor or dermatologist, then let the salon know.

## After colour
- Wait at least 48 hours before the first shampoo.
- Use sulfate-free, colour-safe shampoo and cool or lukewarm water.
- Use heat protectant before ironing or blow-drying. Avoid swimming pools (chlorine) for 1 week.
- Toners and fashion colours fade faster; a toner refresh (₱900 short) every 6–8 weeks helps.

## After rebond, keratin or Brazilian blowout
- Rebond: do not wash, tie, clip or tuck hair behind the ears for **3 days (72 hours)**. Keep it straight and dry.
- Keratin / Brazilian blowout: wait 48 hours before washing; avoid tight ponytails for 2 days.
- Use sulfate-free shampoo to make it last longer. Rebond usually lasts until new hair grows out (roots need retouch after 6–8 months); keratin lasts about 3–4 months.

## After a digital perm
- Do not wash for 48 hours. Scrunch with a curl cream; avoid brushing curls when dry.

## After gel nails
- Avoid very hot water for 24 hours. Use gloves for cleaning chemicals.
- Do not peel off gel; book a soak-off removal to protect your natural nails.

## After waxing and lash services
- Waxing: avoid hot showers, sun exposure and tight clothes on the area for 24 hours.
- Lash lift or extensions: keep lashes dry for 24 hours; no oil-based makeup remover on the lashes.

## Frequently asked questions
- **How often should I get a haircut?** Usually every 6–8 weeks for short styles and every 10–12 weeks to maintain longer hair.
- **Can I colour my hair right after a rebond?** No. Wait at least 2 weeks between rebond and colour.
- **Do you do home service?** No, all services are done at the salon.
- **Can I bring my own hair colour?** No. For safety and results, the salon only uses its own professional colour products. You may bring your own nail polish.
- **Is there a waiting area?** Yes, with free Wi-Fi, water and coffee.
- **Do you cut curly hair?** Yes. Bea and Ate Mara do curly cuts (dry cut available). Same price as a regular haircut.
- **Do you sell hair products?** Yes, a small shelf of shampoo, conditioner, heat protectant and hair oil. Prices are posted at the counter (not in this assistant).
- **Do you have men's grooming?** Men's haircut, men's colour and eyebrow threading. No shaving or beard services.
- **Is the salon open on Mondays?** No, Monday is the rest day.
